**The Problem**

Manual waste sorting is highly inefficient and poses dangerous exposure risks to sanitation workers, especially when handling Hazardous and Toxic (B3) waste.



**The Solution**

Designed a smart trash bin prototype based on Arduino Uno, integrated with multiple sensors to read material characteristics (gas, dielectric, light reflection, metal). Programmed sequential IF-ELSE control logic in C/C++ to autonomously drive 5 servo motors, directing waste into specific compartments.



**The Impact**

\- The system successfully categorized waste into 5 specific categories automatically, effectively eliminating human contact with hazardous (B3) materials.

\- The system's algorithmic innovation received an official \*\*Intellectual Property Right (HKI No. 000602222)\*\* certification from the Ministry of Law and Human Rights of the Republic of Indonesia.
